English

Kodbuzar ("the app") is an offline logic puzzle game. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

  • Data stored on your device only: your game history, scores, chosen name, language, theme and sound/vibration settings are stored locally on your phone so the app can remember them. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • No account is needed and none is created.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs are included, and the app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • Permissions: the app requests vibration (for haptic feedback) and the standard internet permission required by its runtime; it does not send your data anywhere.
  • Children: the app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address. Contact: nuria960315@gmail.com
